Looking after your data

Restoring replaces everything with the backup you pick. Your current data is copied aside first, so a wrong choice is undoable.
Routine backup is the nightly one, or one you took with Back up now — they are written the same way, so they cannot be told apart afterwards. Safety copy was taken automatically just before something changed the database.

Installing adds the Desktop icon for you. The button is here in case you delete it, or moved the folder.

Everything lives in one data.db file. To move this tool to another computer: back up, copy the whole folder across, and press Install there. Your clients, audits and settings come with it.
